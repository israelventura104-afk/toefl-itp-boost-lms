TQB-LIST-0118 — Recording Script
Topic: Dorm Laundry Card / Machine Outage

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a residence hall assistant.

Woman (Student): Hi. The washers on the second floor won't take my laundry card. The reader just blinks red.

Man (RA): Yeah, we've had reports since this morning. Building Services thinks the card readers lost connection after last night's power blip.

Woman: I have a huge pile of clothes and a lab coat I need clean for tomorrow. Is there another floor I can use?

Man: The fourth-floor machines are working. You'll need to take the elevator—the stairwell door by laundry is temporarily locked for a fire-panel test until six.

Woman: Okay. Do I need to reload money on my card, or is the balance still there?

Man: Your balance is fine. Just avoid machines two and three on two—those readers are offline. If a machine takes the card but doesn't start, don't swipe again; come back here and we'll refund the cycle.

Woman: Got it. How long until second floor is fixed?

Man: They said by tomorrow afternoon. I'll post a notice on the laundry door when it's back online.

Woman: Thanks. One more thing—can I borrow a spare laundry basket? Mine cracked.

Man: We have two at the desk. Leave your student ID while you use it, and bring it back tonight.

Woman: Perfect. I'll head to fourth floor now.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
