TQB-LIST-0120 — Recording Script
Topic: Campus Printer Quota / Print Shop

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a staff member at the campus print shop.

Woman (Student): Hi. I tried to print my poster for class, but the system says I've exceeded my print quota.

Man (Staff): Let me check your account. May I see your student ID?

Woman: Sure. I still need twenty color pages today. Is there a way to add more quota?

Man: Yes. Undergraduates get a free black-and-white allotment each term, but color is pay-as-you-go. You can buy a ten-dollar top-up here or online.

Woman: Can I pay with my campus card?

Man: Campus card or credit card both work. After you pay, wait about two minutes for the quota to refresh before you send the job again.

Woman: My professor wants it on eleven-by-seventeen paper. Do you have that in color?

Man: We do, but large color sheets only print on the plotter by the window—not the regular color copiers. I'll unlock that queue after your top-up posts.

Woman: Perfect. One more thing—if the poster looks faded, can I reprint for free?

Man: If it's a machine error, yes—bring the bad copy back the same day and we'll reprint. If you just change the design, you'll use more quota.

Woman: Got it. I'll buy the top-up now.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
