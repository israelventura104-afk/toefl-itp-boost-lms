TQB-LIST-0119 — Recording Script
Topic: Counseling Center Appointment

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a staff member at the counseling center.

Man (Student): Hi. I'd like to make an appointment with a counselor. I've been feeling overwhelmed with midterms.

Woman (Staff): Of course. Is this your first time with us?

Man: Yes. Do I need a referral from my doctor?

Woman: No referral for a first visit. I can offer a twenty-minute intake this week, then a full session after that.

Man: How soon is the intake? My exam is Thursday.

Woman: We have a cancellation tomorrow at eleven, or Friday at nine. Tomorrow fills first.

Man: Tomorrow at eleven, please. Will my professors be told I came here?

Woman: No. Visits are confidential unless there's a safety risk. We don't contact instructors without your written permission.

Man: That's a relief. Should I bring anything?

Woman: Just your student ID, and arrive ten minutes early for forms. If you need same-day crisis help, use the after-hours number on our website—don't wait for the intake.

Man: Okay. I'll see you tomorrow at eleven. Thanks.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
