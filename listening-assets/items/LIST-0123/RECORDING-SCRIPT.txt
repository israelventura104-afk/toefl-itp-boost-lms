TQB-LIST-0123 — Recording Script
Topic: Library Group Study Room Booking

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a library staff member.

Woman (Student): Hi. I need a group study room for four people tomorrow evening. We're preparing a presentation.

Man (Staff): Rooms book in two-hour blocks. I have 6 to 8 p.m. in room 214, or 7 to 9 in room 301—301 has a large display screen.

Woman: 301, please. Do we need to bring our own HDMI cable?

Man: There's a cable in the drawer, but check it when you arrive. If it's missing, borrow one at the desk with your ID.

Woman: What if one person is late? Last time we lost a room for a no-show.

Man: Someone from your group must check in at the desk within ten minutes of the start time, or the reservation is released.

Woman: Can we bring snacks?

Man: Covered drinks are fine. No messy food—crumbs attract pests and you'll lose booking privileges for two weeks.

Woman: Understood. Can I extend the booking if another group doesn't show?

Man: Only at the desk after your first hour, and only if the next slot is free. I can't promise it in advance.

Woman: Okay. Please put it under Maya Chen, ID ending in 4421.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
