TQB-LIST-0121 — Recording Script
Topic: Campus Shuttle Schedule Change

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a campus shuttle driver.

Man (Student): Excuse me. Does this bus still go to the science complex after eight?

Woman (Driver): Not this week. During midterms we're on the exam schedule—after eight we only loop between the library, the dorms, and the student union.

Man: I have a review session in Science Hall at eight-thirty. How do I get there?

Woman: Take the seven-forty from the union to the library, then walk the covered path—it's about six minutes. Or catch the daytime science route; the last one leaves the union at seven-fifty-five.

Man: Seven-fifty-five is tight. Is the covered path lit?

Woman: Yes, and Campus Safety patrols it until midnight during exams. If you miss both options, call the night van—students can request a ride with their ID.

Man: Does the night van cost anything?

Woman: No, it's free for enrolled students. You need to book it at least twenty minutes ahead on the transit app.

Man: Okay. Will normal service to Science Hall resume next week?

Woman: Yes—starting Monday after exams. The change is only for midterm week. Check the app; it shows today's modified map in orange.

Man: Thanks. I'll aim for the seven-fifty-five.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
