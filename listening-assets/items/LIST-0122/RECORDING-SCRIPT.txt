TQB-LIST-0122 — Recording Script
Topic: Club Sports Registration Deadline

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a staff member at the recreation office.

Man (Student): Hi. I want to join the intramural soccer league this semester. Am I too late?

Woman (Staff): Almost. Team rosters close Friday at five, and every player needs a cleared online waiver before then.

Man: I already have a team—we're short one midfielder. Do I register myself or does the captain do it?

Woman: Your captain adds you in the portal, then you complete the waiver and pay the twelve-dollar fee. Until both are done, you won't appear on the roster.

Man: Can I play if my physical exam from last year is still on file?

Woman: For club sports we need a physical from this academic year. The health center can do walk-ins tomorrow morning—bring your ID.

Man: Games are Sundays, right? I work until noon.

Woman: Most divisions start at two, but check your pool schedule after Friday. Late adds sometimes get placed in the evening division.

Man: Okay. If I miss Friday, is there a waitlist?

Woman: There's a waitlist for free-agent players, not for full teams. If your team is incomplete by Friday, the whole team is dropped.

Man: Got it. I'll finish the waiver tonight and book the physical for tomorrow.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
