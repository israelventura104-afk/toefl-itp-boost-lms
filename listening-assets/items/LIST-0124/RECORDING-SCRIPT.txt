TQB-LIST-0124 — Recording Script
Topic: Career Fair Employer Check-In

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a staff member at the campus career fair.

Woman (Student): Hi. I'm here for the career fair. Doors open at ten, right? I wanted to get a good spot in line for the tech companies.

Man (Staff): Students can enter at ten, but employer booths don't start conversations until ten-thirty. You can check in now and pick up a name badge.

Woman: Do I need to register online first?

Man: If you already registered, just scan your QR code at that table. Walk-ups are allowed, but some employers only see pre-registered students for interviews.

Woman: I'm in business casual. Is that okay, or should I run back for a blazer?

Man: Business casual is fine for most booths. Engineering and startups are more relaxed; consulting tables prefer jackets, but you won't be turned away.

Woman: Where do I drop paper resumes if a company isn't taking them at the booth?

Man: Use the resume drop boxes along the back wall—they're labeled by industry. Don't leave stacks on the tables; staff clear those.

Woman: Is there a bag check? I brought a backpack with my laptop.

Man: Yes, free bag check by the east entrance. Keep your phone and a few resumes with you. The fair closes at three; interviews upstairs run until five.

Woman: Perfect. I'll check in and grab a badge.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
