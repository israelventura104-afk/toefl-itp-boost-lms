TQB-LIST-0116 — Recording Script
Topic: Campus ID Card Replacement

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a staff member at the campus card office.

Man (Student): Hi. I think I lost my campus ID. I had it yesterday in the dining hall, but it's not in my wallet now.

Woman (Staff): Okay. First, I can deactivate the old card so nobody else can use your meal plan or building access.

Man: Please do that. How long does a replacement take?

Woman: If you have another photo ID with you, we can print a new card today—usually in about twenty minutes. There's a ten-dollar replacement fee.

Man: I have my driver's license. Can I pay with a card?

Woman: Yes, debit or credit. Also, check the lost-and-found desk in the student union. Dining hall staff sometimes turn IDs in there within a day.

Man: I already called them—nothing yet. I'll go ahead and replace it. I need library access tonight.

Woman: Understood. Stand on the marks for the photo, and I'll deactivate the old number now. Your new card will work as soon as it's printed.

Man: Great. One more question—does this affect my bus pass?

Woman: The campus shuttle is linked to your student account, not the physical card number, so that stays active. Only door access and meal swipes move to the new card.

Man: Perfect. Thanks for walking me through it.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
