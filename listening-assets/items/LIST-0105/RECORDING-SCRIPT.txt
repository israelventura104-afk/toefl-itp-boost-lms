TQB-LIST-0105 — Recording Script
Topic: Writing Center Appointment

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a writing tutor.

Woman (Student): Hi. I'd like to make an appointment at the Writing Center. I've got a research paper due next Friday.

Man (Tutor): Sure. Have you already written a draft, or are you still outlining?

Woman: I have a rough draft, but my professor said the thesis is too broad and the sources aren't strong enough.

Man: That's a common issue. We can look at both. Sessions are forty-five minutes. Do mornings or afternoons work better?

Woman: Afternoons. I have labs until one every day this week.

Man: I have an opening tomorrow at two-thirty, or Thursday at three. Tomorrow fills up fast, though.

Woman: Tomorrow at two-thirty works. Should I bring anything besides the draft?

Man: Yes—bring the assignment sheet and a list of the sources you're using. Also, upload the draft to our portal tonight so I can skim it before we meet.

Woman: I can do that. One more thing: can we focus on citations? I'm never sure about APA format.

Man: Absolutely. We'll tighten the thesis first, then spend the last part of the session on APA. See you tomorrow at two-thirty.

Woman: Great. Thanks.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
