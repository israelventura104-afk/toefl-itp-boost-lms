TQB-LIST-0125 — Recording Script
Topic: International Student Visa Document Pickup

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and an advisor at the international student office.

Man (Student): Hi. I'm here to pick up my updated I-20. I travel home next week for the break.

Woman (Advisor): Sure. May I see your student ID and passport? I also need you to sign the travel endorsement page.

Man: Here they are. Do I need a new visa stamp, or is this enough?

Woman: If your visa stamp is still valid, you only need the travel signature on the I-20. Yours looks valid through next August.

Man: I'll be gone three weeks. Should I report my travel dates in the portal?

Woman: Yes—enter your departure and return dates before you leave. If your return flight changes, update it within five days of landing.

Man: Can I work on campus during the break if I come back early?

Woman: On-campus work is fine during the official break as long as you remain enrolled for next term. Off-campus work still needs separate authorization.

Man: Understood. Is there anything else I should carry at the airport?

Woman: Carry your passport, I-20, and proof of enrollment—an unofficial transcript printout works. Keep digital copies too, but officers usually want paper.

Man: Great. I'll sign and update the portal tonight.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
