TQB-LIST-0117 — Recording Script
Topic: Chemistry Lab Equipment Checkout

Narrator: Listen to a conversation between a student and a chemistry lab supervisor.

Woman (Student): Excuse me. I'm in Professor Kim's afternoon lab, and I need to check out a digital balance and two graduated cylinders.

Man (Supervisor): Sure. Are you on the lab roster for today? I'll need your student ID and the experiment number from the syllabus.

Woman: Here's my ID. It's experiment six—the titration lab. My partner is already saving a bench by the window.

Man: Good. Balances can't leave the instrument room, so you'll weigh samples here and carry only the labeled vials back to your bench.

Woman: Got it. Do the cylinders have to be returned washed, or can we leave them in the rinse rack?

Man: Rinse them at your sink, then place them on the drying rack by the window—not the dirty cart. We inventory everything before five.

Woman: What if the balance reading jumps around? Last week mine wouldn't stabilize.

Man: Close the draft doors, and wait for the green light. If it still drifts, move to balance three—balance two is out for calibration until Thursday.

Woman: Okay. Can I also get a pair of heat-resistant gloves for the hot plate?

Man: Yes, but sign the glove log. Damaged gloves go in the red bin; don't put them back on the shelf.

Woman: Understood. Thanks—I'll bring the cylinders back before five.

Narrator: Now get ready to answer the questions.
