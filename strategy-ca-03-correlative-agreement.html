Correlative Agreement one skill

With either...or and neither...nor, match the verb to the subject nearest it.

1 Teach2 Demo3 Practice

Today

Learn the nearest-subject rule, try three together, then practice ten on your own.

Goal today: identify the pair, then let the noun closest to the verb control agreement.

1. Find the pairEither...or, neither...nor, or both...and.
2. Find the nearer nounIt decides singular or plural.

High-yield patterns

  • Either...or → agree with the nearer subject.
    Either the instructor or the students are responsible.
  • Neither...nor → agree with the nearer subject.
    Neither the students nor the instructor is available.
  • Both...and → plural.
    Both the lecture and the workshop are required.
  • As well as is an interruption, not a correlative pair.
    The instructor, as well as the students, is ready.
Remember

The first subject may be a trap. Look immediately before the verb.
